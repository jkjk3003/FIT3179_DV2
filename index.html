Preserving the Past, Building the Future

An exploration of Australia's architectural heritage and modern housing booms.

1. The Geography of Australian Heritage

Density and Protection

Australia is a vast continent, but its historical footprint is unevenly distributed. When adjusting for population, island states like Tasmania show a highly concentrated density of historical sites (Graph 2).

However, when looking at the physical land protected (Graph 5), mainland states like New South Wales dedicate massive hectares of land to heritage conservation.
